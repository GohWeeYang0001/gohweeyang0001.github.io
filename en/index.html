FAQ

Does my pet need vaccination/deworming before grooming?
We recommend pets to have completed basic vaccination and regular deworming for safety. Please inform us in advance if there are special conditions.
Do I need to prepare anything?
No preparation required. We bring all professional grooming tools and cleaning supplies. Just provide a space for grooming.
Do you need to use our water or electricity?
Yes, we will need access to your home’s water and power supply. If not available, please inform us beforehand.
